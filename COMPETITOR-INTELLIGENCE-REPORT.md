## Competitor Deep Dives

### 1. Trisul — Elev Alto do Ipiranga ⚠️ PRIMARY THREAT
**Why:** Most directly comparable positioning — "condomínio clube MCMV" near metro in Ipiranga. Dedicated landing page (elevaltodoipiranga.com.br) and dedicated Facebook property page confirm significant paid media investment.

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Address | Rua Coronel Domingos Ferreira, 205 — Alto do Ipiranga |
| Price From | ~R$320.000 (37m², suíte, varanda) |
| Metro | 400m — Metrô Santos-Imigrantes (Linha Verde) |
| Leisure | 30+ áreas comuns (adult + children pool, gym, 3 party halls, coworking, sports bar, cinema, pet care, laundry, orchard, hammock area) |
| Faixa | 3 (R$4.400–R$8.000 family income) |
| Entry | Zero with FGTS; ITBI + registro gratuitos |

**Key ad copy angles:**
- "Mais de 30 áreas comuns" — amenity quantity leadership
- "A 400 metros do metrô" — proximity precision
- "Zero entrada, ITBI e registro grátis" — total cost of entry
- "Lazer de clube, preço de MCMV"
- "Elev — Para quem quer subir na vida" (aspirational brand tone)

**Versus Direcional:** Trisul wins on amenity count (30+ vs. 10+) and metro distance (400m vs. 700m). Direcional wins on price (~R$216K vs. ~R$320K), income accessibility (HIS/F1–2 vs. F3), and unit size (41–53m² vs. 34–37m²).
